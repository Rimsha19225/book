Contact & Support

Getting Help​

If you need assistance with this textbook or encounter any issues, we're here to help.

Technical Support​

For technical issues related to the textbook platform:

  • GitHub Issues: Report bugs or request features on our GitHub repository
  • Email: Contact our support team at support@physical-ai-textbook.org

Content Questions​

For questions about the textbook content:

  • Use the integrated AI chat assistant (click the blue button in the bottom-right corner)
  • The AI assistant can answer questions about the current chapter and provide context-aware responses

Community Support​

Join our community for discussions and peer support:

  • Discord: Join our robotics and AI discussion server
  • Forum: Participate in our online community forum
  • Office Hours: Weekly virtual office hours (schedule TBD)

Reporting Issues​

If you find errors in the content or encounter technical problems:

  1. Check the FAQ below to see if your issue is already addressed
  2. Use the search function to see if others have encountered similar issues
  3. If you can't find a solution, contact us using the methods above

Feedback​

We value your feedback to improve this textbook:

  • Rate chapters that were particularly helpful
  • Suggest additional topics or improvements
  • Share your success stories and projects

Frequently Asked Questions​

Q: How do I access the AI chat assistant? A: Click the blue floating button in the bottom-right corner of any page.

Q: Is this textbook free to use? A: Yes, this textbook is completely free and open-source.

Q: Can I use this textbook offline? A: The interactive features require an internet connection, but you can download content for offline reading.

Q: How often is the content updated? A: We regularly update the content to reflect the latest developments in Physical AI and robotics.

System Requirements​

To use this textbook effectively:

  • Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
  • Stable internet connection for AI assistant features
  • At least 2GB of RAM (4GB recommended for simulation activities)
  • JavaScript enabled

Accessibility​

We are committed to making this textbook accessible to all learners:

  • Screen reader compatible
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • High contrast mode available
  • Text size adjustment options

For immediate assistance, use the AI chat assistant by clicking the blue button in the bottom-right corner.
